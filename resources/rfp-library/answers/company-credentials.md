## Case studies — B2B SaaS / WordPress→headless
Pick 3 based on the closest profile match to the prospect:
- **Lano** — B2B SaaS (global payroll), WordPress→headless: 1,000+ pages
  migrated in 5 days, 4 languages, ~1 hour to full team adoption.
- **Spendesk** — French B2B SaaS (spend management), left a previous
  headless CMS + HubSpot: 80% faster page creation, 25% smaller codebase,
  1,000+ posts migrated in 90 days, 5 languages, 2x faster CMS navigation.
- **Emarsys** (SAP-owned) — B2B SaaS, direct WordPress→headless brand-site
  migration delivered in 3 weeks, atomic-design component library.
  Strongest "leaving WordPress" reference specifically.
- **Ponant** — luxury travel, WordPress + Magento→headless: 83% reduction
  in time-to-market, marketing manages 80% of content independently,
  multi-locale/multi-site. Good non-SaaS multi-locale proof point.
- **Unified** — 84% LCP improvement post-rebuild. Use for Core Web Vitals
  questions, always labelled illustrative rather than guaranteed.

## References (contactable) vs case studies (public links)
These are **different asks** and an RFP often requests both. Public case
study links do not satisfy a "two contactable references" requirement —
that needs a named, willing customer plus account-team permission. The
validated approach: give the two closest-profile references with a real
"why this maps to you" paragraph each, and link the public case-study
index for breadth.
