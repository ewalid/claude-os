# people.md (template)

This is the tracked, agnostic template. The real `resources/people.md`
is git-ignored (it holds real colleague names — operator-specific data
that stays local). `darwin-setup` writes the real one from this shape.

Reference on the people the operator works with, and their preferences.
Update as Darwin learns more through daily-briefing, process-customer,
and direct correction from the operator.

## The operator's pod
(People whose deals/accounts the operator supports but doesn't own —
unclaimed requests tied to them are "worth claiming?" flags, never
tasks. For a sales-engineering role these are typically Account
Executives; adapt the label to the operator's actual org.)

- **<Name>** — <role>. Notes: <need validation>.

## Peer colleagues (NOT the pod above)
(Same team / same kind of work as the operator. If one of these has
already replied to or claimed a request, it's handled — never flag it
as "worth claiming.")

- **<Name>** — <role/specialty>.

## Management
- **<Name>** — the operator's manager. Context, not a flag source.

## Status
Fill in during darwin-setup; mark unconfirmed entries "need validation".
